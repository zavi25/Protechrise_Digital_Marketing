**What I Built**
I developed a comprehensive 30-day digital marketing strategy and content calendar for AYOKEL Global Supplies Ltd, 
an agricultural sourcing and supply business. It includes target audience analysis, SMART marketing objectives, 
recommended digital platforms, content pillars, daily post topics, captions, calls to action, posting schedules, 
advertising budget, and performance indicators (KPIs).

**How to Run It**
Open the editable Word document and review the content calendar. Create the required graphics and videos using Canva or 
similar tools, then schedule posts on Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn using Meta Business Suite and each platform's 
scheduling tools. Manage customer enquiries through WhatsApp Business, track performance weekly, and adjust the 
strategy based on engagement, leads, and conversions.
